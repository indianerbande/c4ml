# Material Symbols asset source

The five SVG files in this directory are exact, unmodified copies of Google
Material Symbols Outlined at 24 px.

- Upstream: <https://github.com/google/material-design-icons>
- Pinned commit: `84ccef280841abfac506afc4ad4a2782f6d0a1d0`
- License: Apache License 2.0; the adjacent `LICENSE` is an unchanged copy of
  the upstream license and is packaged beside this notice.
- Retrieved: 2026-08-30

The selected symbols are `folder_open`, `schema`, `download`, `menu_book`, and
`settings`. They are presentation assets for the local workbench activity bar;
they are not used in authored diagrams or exported SVG/PNG.
